4 · What green means

Today: green carries 9 meanings

  • primary CTA fill (Start, End match, Rematch)
  • bottom-nav and tab selection
  • text links (TOURNAMENT →, QUICK ▸, FILTERS +, Edit Rules)
  • LIVE badge (but LIVE is red in 4 other places)
  • lead (classic board numeral + underline)
  • success / saved notice
  • FINAL badge (identical to LIVE)
  • decoration: landing numerals, tags, green offset shadows, GUEST MODE chip
  • input focus outline

Proposed: green means "go" and "live", nothing else

  • Go: the fill of the single primary forward action on a screen. Always a filled button with a white label (5.78:1) and an ink border/shadow.
  • Live: the LIVE chip (word + dot + optional pulse) while a match is actually broadcasting.
  • Never: lead, selection, links, focus, success, final, decoration, shadows, borders, dialogs.
  • Selection = ink inversion. Links = ink + underline. Focus = ink ring. Lead = the numbers (and team colour on team boards).
