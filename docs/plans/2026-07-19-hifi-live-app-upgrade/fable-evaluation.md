Evaluator: Fable 5 (senior design/orchestration review). Date: 2026-07-19.

**What I inspected:** eval-hifi-reference.md and eval-current-app.md in full; discovery.json in full (60 screens + identity split + 14 gaps); decomposition.json in full (14 epics, 82 issues, prOrder, crossCutting); flagged-designs.json (structure + consistency check against the 60 verdicts); screen-designs.json — full structural scan of all 60 entries (verdict fields, scope, issue counts, design lengths) plus deep spot-checks of 15 screens spanning every group: Guest landing, Play hub, Cricket toss, Goals scorer, Sets scorer, Generic live game, Tournament match score, In-match ⋯ menu, Spectator—cricket, Result, Match detail, Standings, Settings/More, Your profile, My teams, Legacy orphans, Dev showcases. ## A. Findings soundness — SOUND, two nits

**HiFi reference inventory (eval-hifi-reference.md): excellent.** Palette extracted with frequency counts, the two-family typography, the 99px/50%/10–16px radii system, colored-glow shadows, the three keyframes, and — most importantly — the *behavioral* HiFi traits (question-asking micro-copy, attribution mini-flows with Skip+Confirm, coaching chips, team-only mode, share-QR ecosystem). The "Could NOT determine" section is honest about what was label-inventoried vs pixel-traced. Critically, the blend correctly does **not** import Hanken Grotesk/DM Mono — typography identity (Inter + JetBrains Mono) is held constant everywhere, which is the right call.

**Current-app inventory (eval-current-app.md): accurate where I verified it.** `--shadow-2xs: 3px 3px 0px 0.5px hsl(0 0% 0%/.35)`, `--radius: 0.25rem`, Inter/JetBrains Mono, `@custom-variant dark` with zero `.dark` rules, and no gold/green-wash tokens — all confirmed in `src/index.css`. The design-debt section correctly notes several I-xxx items already carry fix-comments in code.

Two nits:
1. **Nav-tab mischaracterization:** eval-current-app says app shell tabs are "Play / History / Stats" (index.jsx:566-582); the My-teams verifier, with line citations (index.jsx:564-591), says there are **5 items: Home/Play/History/Stats/Account** (Account conditional on cloudAuthAvailable). The verifier's version should be treated as authoritative; the BottomTabNav issue's "5→target-tab IA" wording suggests decomposition already absorbed the correction, but the findings doc should be fixed so nobody plans against 3 tabs.
2. **`--shadow` is not fully zero-blur:** the actual token is `3px 3px 0px 0.5px …, 3px 1px 2px -0.5px …` — a second, slightly-blurred layer. crossCutting item 14 catches this ("--shadow-md is not zero-blur"); fine, but the identity description "hard offset, ZERO blur" is 95% true, not 100%.

## B. Iteration quality — REAL WORK, with a localized verify failure on 3–4 screens

**The verify stage did genuine adversarial work on 56–57 of 60 screens.** Evidence: verdicts routinely cite file:line (startMatch at MonoQuickMatch:801, battingTeam hardcode at :819, side-out early-return at MonoSetsLiveScore:220-223, z-260 backdrop at index.jsx:830), catch **false "keeps"** (the claimed "existing 3px 3px 0 hard shadow" on Play-hub tiles and Match-detail cards does NOT exist — it's soft/blurred today; verify correctly reclassifies it as *new* work, and crossCutting 14 institutionalizes this), catch wrong premises (convex/teams.ts DOES exist; NewUserFlow is NOT an orphan — do-not-delete), and catch data-model holes (history entries carry only a flattened score string; no MVP signal exists). This is exactly what an adversarial pass should produce.

**However, the verify stage silently failed on 3 screens and the design stage on 1:**
- **Goals scorer** — verdict.issues = `["placeholder"]` (yet scopeVerdict says "too-big" — that verdict is unbacked)
- **Generic live game view** — keepsBrutalist, adoptsHifi AND verdict.issues are all `"placeholder"`
- **Your profile (account)** — verdict.issues = `["placeholder"]` (scopeVerdict "too-big" unbacked)
- **Tournament match score** — blendDesign is literally `"See above."` (structured fields are populated and its verdict has 7 real issues, but the design narrative is missing)

So the headline signals are overstated: it is **0/57 diluted**, not 0/60, and 2 of the 42 "too-big" flags (Goals scorer, Your profile) have no supporting reasoning. The Goals scorer is the flagship 7-sport scorer — shipping it on an unverified design is the single biggest quality hole in the package. The decompose stage partially compensated (its Goals/Profile issues reference I-051/I-052, max-height:540px compaction, conditional social chips — real code knowledge), but compensation is not verification.

**The 3 "no HiFi win" flags are correct.** Play hub (the plain-language prompts already exist in code — restyle, not new guidance), Legacy orphans (pure delete/reconcile; every "adoptsHifi" item is retention), Dev showcases (dev-gated route; the win is DX, not user UX). All three verdicts show their reasoning and it holds.

**Is the 82-issue split enough for the 42 too-big flags? Mostly yes — the splitting logic is genuinely good (extraction-before-restyle, backend-before-UI, primitive-before-consumer) — but at least 6 issues still openly contain multiple PRs in one issue:**

## C. Blend integrity — ACHIEVED, with a governance gap

The blend philosophy is consistently right and, notably, **bidirectional**: hard shell / soft content on scoring surfaces (Sets/Goals keep `.mono-arena-half` untouched and wrap warmth around it), boxy "competition context" chrome on tournament surfaces, and — impressively — *brutalizing* screens that had drifted soft (Sport home "brutalize the currently-Swiss spine"; Tournament list "add hard offset shadow to the currently-flat Swiss cards"; showcases killing ad-hoc #fafafa/#dcfce7). Off-brand #0066ff is hunted down in onboarding, tournaments, orphans, showcases. Green-for-lead-only and gold-for-milestone-only discipline appears everywhere.

Where the blend gets closest to going wrong — and, to their credit, the verify/decompose stages caught each locally:
- **Cricket toss** — verdict: "closest-to-dilution screen"; soft elements concentrated (coin, circles, pills, sheet, wash, gold, pulse). Decomposition dropped the tappable coin and made gold static. Good.
- **In-match ⋯ menu** — sheet content risks "generic friendly iOS sheet"; identity rests on the frame alone.
- **My teams** — self-noted pile-up (crests + washes + gold + capsules + chips); constrained to one gold + two greens.
- **Standings leader breathing pulse** — verdict called it decoration cutting against brutalism; decomposition dropped it. Correct.
- **Play hub** — softness with thin UX gain (the flagged no-win screen); kept as restyle-only, correctly.

**The gap:** this softness-budget discipline is enforced screen-by-screen in prose, with no single normative rule. Ten different issues re-derive "constrain gold to one accent." The Foundation tokens issue should own a short **blend-governance contract** (green = lead/live only; gold = ONE milestone accent per screen; capsule/circle = interactive-only, never containers; hard shell/soft content; breathing pulse = live-state only, reduced-motion gated) so build agents inherit it instead of rediscovering it.

## D. Decomposition & sequencing — STRONG STRUCTURE, four defects

**Structure:** 14 epics, 82 issues, prOrder complete and internally consistent (I verified: prOrder ⊆ issues and issues ⊆ prOrder, no duplicate titles, no dangling dependsOn). Foundation-first integrity is real: tokens → sheet/chips/toggle/avatars/empty-state/nav (positions 0–6), then extraction chores (orphans, MonoQuickSetup lift) before restyles, backend trunks before UI leaves (live stack → snapshot query → primitives → spectator screens; friends backend → social screens; team model → team screens; stats model → attribution; event log → graphs). All missing/stub surfaces named in the brief are covered: Result/Scorecard/Share (5 issues), Settings (4 + dark-mode token issue), Social (5), Teams (6), Spectator/Live (9). The crossCutting list is the best artifact in the package — it captures the false-keeps problem, the canonical-worktree ambiguity, and the inert-toggle traps.

**Defects:**
1. **DROPPED SCREEN — In-match ⋯ menu.** It has a full design and a substantive 7-issue verdict (including a concrete descope-to-one-PR recipe), MonoSheet's description names "in-match menu" as a consumer, and the Share-QR issue's prBoundary says "⋯ trigger wiring" — but **no issue anywhere builds the ⋯ button or the menu sheet**. The Share-QR issue depends on a trigger that nothing creates. This is a real coverage hole, not cosmetic.
2. **prOrder violation:** "Tournament match fixture ribbon + advance-to-final finish" (pos 35) depends on "Tournament dispatch shell extraction + blue-to-token migration" (pos 49). As ordered, the ribbon PR starts before its prerequisite exists.
3. **Epic screens-array bookkeeping:** "Goals scorer (…7 sports)" (name mismatch — epic says "Goals scorer"), "In-match ⋯ menu" (dropped), and "Undo last ball / correction affordance" (issue exists, screens array omits it) are not covered by any epic's screens list. Minor for the first/third, symptom of the real drop for the second.
4. **BottomTabNav timing tension:** the IA rework (Home·Play·History·Friends/More) sits at position 6, but Friends (P3, pos ~63) and Settings/More (pos ~57) don't exist for dozens of PRs. The Settings verdict correctly calls the 6th-tab/fold question "a product decision, not just code." As sequenced, the nav either ships dead tabs or ships the component without the IA change — the issue's "degrade gracefully" hand-waves this. Split it explicitly: PR now = shared nav component + I-066 label fix on the CURRENT 5-tab IA; the Friends/More tab-swap = a later, product-approved PR gated on those surfaces existing.

**Design-debt kernel issues:** handled sensibly — I-066/I-086/I-053 are folded into their screen issues, and crossCutting 12 says verify-open-status-first. Two strengthenings needed: (a) eval-current-app shows I-044/I-047/I-049/I-051/I-052/I-013 already have shipped fix-comments in code, and my `bd search` for these IDs returns empty — the I-xxx registry is NOT in beads, so "check the kernel" currently has no defined mechanism. Make the verification an explicit first task (locate the registry, mark fixed items, file only residuals) rather than a prose warning, or the build agents WILL file dupes. (b) I-068 (green 4/6 accent) appears in the debt list but no scorer issue explicitly resolves keep-vs-remove; the Cricket LO blend issue should state the decision (the code comment defends it as "the single documented green accent" — likely keep, but decide once).

## E. Risks & gaps

1. **Greenfield scale is the dominant risk, and it's honestly represented:** five backend trunks (live stack promotion, public snapshot, player-stats model, event log, friends + teams models) gate ~30 of 82 issues. The plan correctly refuses to fake data (honest empty states on Spectator-football, no-fake-data Activity feed). But 82 issues at ~53 M / 23 L is realistically **90+ PRs**; without milestone cut-lines the tail (social/teams, positions 62–82) will drift. Declare 3 shippable milestones: M1 = foundation + play/setup + scorers + match-end (user-visible transformation), M2 = live/spectator + share, M3 = social + teams. This also creates natural re-planning checkpoints.
2. **Canonical-worktree ambiguity** (`.worktrees/live-matches` vs `.claude/worktrees/wf_5fe79697`) is flagged in the land-live-stack issue but is a *decision*, not a task — someone must diff the two and pick before that PR starts. Assign it explicitly.
3. **Verify's factual-slip harvest must survive into build briefs.** crossCutting 14 exists, but the per-screen corrections (999px not 99px; nonexistent shadows; AppScoringPrompt is a centered modal, not a sheet; sportConfig has no `target` field) live only in screen-designs.json verdicts. Build-agent briefs must carry the relevant verdict's issues verbatim, or Opus executors will re-import the original designs' errors.
4. **A11y is under-specified as a gate.** Individual verdicts demand focus-trap parity, role=img, role=alert retention, reduced-motion — but no epic-level acceptance criterion says "a11yContracts.test.js green + reduced-motion audit per PR." Cheap to add, expensive to retrofit.
5. **Workflow hygiene:** the placeholder verdicts (B above) mean the verify stage can fail silently. If this workflow runs again, add a schema check that rejects `"placeholder"`/`"See above."` content — it cost nothing to catch programmatically.
