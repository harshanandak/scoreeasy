The match has a place. Show it.

Behind the score sits the venue the game is played in: a floodlit stadium, an indoor hall, a local ground. The court is laid out in true perspective from regulation dimensions. Each team's fans sit on its own side of the stand, wearing its colour because the art is drawn into the team blend itself. The dark theme is a floodlit night match; the light theme is the same ground in the afternoon.

The venue reacts to the match. When a team goes on a run, a Mexican wave rolls through its fans. On set point and match point, the floodlights flare. Your photo turns any picture of your own court into the backdrop.

The art never carries colour of its own. It can only lighten or darken the team colour, and the engine solves, team by team, how far it may go so the numerals still clear 8:1 on the brightest pixel. The names sit in a clean lane. That makes it legible courtside in sunlight, whatever is drawn or dropped in.
