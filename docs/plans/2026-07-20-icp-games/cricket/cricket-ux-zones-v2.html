How to score a ball here

Tap the field where the ball actually went — one tap records a single, and the spot you touch is saved as the placement, so the wagon wheel fills in for free. Double-tap for a four, swipe out past the rope for a six, and tap the dot pad when nothing is scored. Long-press anywhere to open the wicket sheet.

Reading the numbers up top: RRR is the run rate Mumbai still needs — 8.60 an over — while CRR is how fast they're actually going (8.48). P'ship is what this pair has added together so far, and Econ is the runs a bowler gives away each over.
