# Score Easy

## Register

product

## Users

A scorer records a live sports match, often one-handed on a phone beside a court
or field in bright daylight and with unreliable connectivity. Spectators can
view a match when the scorer shares it. Harsha is the product owner.

## Product Purpose

Choose a sport, set up a match, score it, correct mistakes, and review the result.
A point must survive refresh, backgrounding, and a failed network connection.
Score, serve, sets or innings, and undo history must agree.

## Brand Personality

The current visual preference is Refined Soft: a cleaner overall app, retaining
selected clearly defined controls from the Mono comparison. This comes from
Harsha's 2026-10-21 direction; the cross-screen proposal still needs visual review.

## Anti-references

Do not apply the Mono comparison's heavy frames and hard offset shadows to every
surface. Do not restore the redundant names, serving cues, muddy scrims, or
out-of-reach Undo from the earlier scorer explorations.

## Design Principles

- Scoring and correction stay in one-thumb reach.
- Show each piece of match information once, with a clear hierarchy.
- A team's colour identifies that team; it does not indicate who is leading.
- Use one visual vocabulary across setup, scoring, dialogs, and the app shell.
- Preserve match data and privacy while changing presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Touch controls are at least 44px. Keep visible keyboard focus, accessible names,
readable outdoor contrast, and reduced-motion support. Check 360px and 390px phone
layouts and difficult team-colour pairs such as yellow against white. Colour
must not be the only indication of serve, selection, or match state.
